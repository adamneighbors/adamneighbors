About

I help teams ship dependable software around ERP and operations—custom Odoo work, Python services, SQL-heavy data flows, and automation that stays observable in production. After more than a decade building and maintaining systems across manufacturing, utilities, and home services, I care most about clarity: code people can own, integrations that fail loudly, and delivery that matches how work actually runs.

I work day to day on Linux—desktop, servers, and containers—and I’m most productive in the shell and the usual ops toolchain. It’s my preferred environment for development and for running the systems I build; I still touch Windows when a stack or team requires it.
